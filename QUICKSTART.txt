EVERY DAY (3-5 minutes):

  cd ~/AI-Workspace/Career/shiva-job-search
  ./daily.sh

That's it. Runs:
  1. Scrapes fresh jobs from 7+ sources
  2. Scores each job (AI fit + keyword match)
  3. Launches dashboard at http://localhost:9009

THEN IN THE DASHBOARD:

  Browse        → Jobs Feed tab, filtered by source & industry
  Evaluate      → Check fit label (click to see raw scores)
  Research      → Click "Research + Tailor" to get company brief & contacts
  Apply         → Click "Mark Applied" to hide from feed and track status
  Track         → Applied tab to manage your pipeline

STOP THE DASHBOARD:

  Press Ctrl+C in the terminal

SEE DETAILED GUIDE:

  cat DAILY-WORKFLOW.md

FULL COMMANDS (if you need them):

  ./daily.sh                           # Full daily run (recommended)
  python3 cli.py scrape --skip-google  # Scrape only (faster)
  python3 cli.py score                 # Score only (if you updated profile)
  python3 cli.py refilter --dry-run    # Check relevance gate
